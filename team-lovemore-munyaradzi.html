Background

Professional profile.

Lovemore holds a B Arch degree from the National University of Science and Technology (Zimbabwe) and is registered with SACAP as PrArch 21932. He initially worked as a technician for over 10 years across commercial, industrial, health and residential projects, with experience in design development, working drawings and site supervision.

He attained his Bachelor of Architecture Degree in 2008 and has worked as an Architect since then, leading project teams and carrying experience through conceptual grounding, construction and completion. He was instrumental in forming KALM.Ntg Architects (Pty) Ltd in 2012.
