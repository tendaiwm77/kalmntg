Background

Professional profile.

James holds a B Arch degree from the National University of Science and Technology (Zimbabwe), graduating in 2006, and is registered with SACAP as Pr Arch 21097. He has worked across commercial, industrial, health and residential projects, including design development, working drawings and site supervision.

He has worked as an Architect and team leader, applying experience to conceptual design and construction. James joined KALM.Ntg Architects (Pty) Ltd as an associate in 2016.
